📊 Amazon Prime Movies & TV Shows Analysis Dashboard

This project is a Power BI Dashboard created to analyze Amazon Prime Movies and TV Shows data using basic data analytics and visualization concepts. The dashboard provides meaningful insights into content distribution, genres, ratings, release trends, and country-wise availability of shows on Amazon Prime Video.

🔍 Business Problem

Streaming platforms like Amazon Prime contain thousands of movies and TV shows across multiple genres, countries, and audience categories. Analyzing such large datasets helps businesses understand:

Which genres attract the most viewers
What type of content is most available
Audience rating distribution
Growth trends of movies and TV shows over time
Country-wise content contribution

This dashboard helps transform raw entertainment data into actionable insights for better content understanding and decision-making.

📌 Key Features
Ratings analysis of total shows
Genre-wise content distribution
Movies vs TV Shows comparison
Country-wise total shows visualization
Release year trend analysis
Interactive and visually appealing dashboard design  

📈 Meaningful Insights
Drama is the most dominant genre on Amazon Prime.
Most content falls under the 13+ and 16+ ratings category, showing strong focus on teen and adult audiences.
Movies contribute around 81% of total content, while TV Shows account for only 19%.
Content releases increased rapidly after the year 2000, showing the platform’s massive expansion in recent years.
Countries like the United States and India contribute significantly to the platform’s content library.
